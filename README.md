# Gathering Is Real

**One minute. Change a life.**

Gathering Is Real is a non-profit that connects people seeking purpose with meaningful service opportunities around the world. We believe everyone can make a difference, even in *one* minute, and our mission is to make serving simple so more light reaches more lives.

## Our Story

Gathering Is Real was founded in April 2026 by Brooklyn Daly and Maddisyn Jolley, who serve as its Directors. Both served 18-month missions for The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. Since returning home in 2025, they have felt a strong desire to serve more, and with a lot of prayer they felt inspired to start organizing service projects.

The first project began with a conversation on an airplane. A fellow traveler shared his connection to Guam and his plans to return there, which sparked the idea of finding a meaningful way to serve that community. That conversation became Gathering Is Real's first service project.

## What We Aim To Do

- **Make serving simple.** Many people want to help but don't know where to start. We lower the barrier so that a single minute is enough to take part.
- **Serve places that matter to people.** People around the world share places that hold special meaning to them. We work to find meaningful ways to serve there.
- **Raise awareness of overlooked places.** We want to draw attention to communities that don't get as much attention as they deserve.
- **Give more people an outlet to serve.** We want anyone looking for more purpose to have a way to bring light into someone else's life.

## How It Makes an Impact

Anyone can take part in three ways:

1. **Write a note.** Send a kind, encouraging note to the people we are serving. We carry these notes with us to the community of the current project.
2. **Recommend a place.** Tell us about a place that matters to you, either by name or by selecting a country on our world map. These recommendations help decide where we serve next.
3. **Donate.** Give through Venmo or Zeffy. Our commitment is that every dollar goes straight into the project it supports.

People can also follow the current project, sign up to be notified when a new one is announced, and follow our work on Instagram, Facebook and YouTube.

## Our Work So Far

### Guam: Guam Memorial Hospital

Our first project responded to a major typhoon in April 2026. We raised **$302** to donate towels and linens to Guam Memorial Hospital, helping care for the sick during the storm's aftermath. Photos and video from the project are on the site, and Guam is marked with a star on our world map as a place we have already served.

### What's next

We are lining up where we serve next. Our current project page will share the need, who we are helping, and when, as soon as it is announced.
